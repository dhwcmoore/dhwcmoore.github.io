01

Engineer-trusted automation

Automation should produce structured proposals that can be inspected, reproduced, tested, and revised rather than opaque conclusions.

02

Independent verification

The component that proposes an answer should not be the sole authority that accepts it. Trust boundaries must be explicit and fail closed.

03

Constructive diagnostics

When a baseline is inconsistent or incomplete, a useful tool should return a witness that explains the defect, not merely a red status light.

Research programme

Pattern, rupture, and warrant

The wider programme asks how organised patterns persist, fail, and become reconfigured across changing material, relational, and symbolic media, and how formal systems can state exactly what a passage has preserved without pretending that it has preserved everything that matters.

For engineering, this becomes a practical discipline: keep the conclusion, its provenance, its verification conditions, and its unresolved obligations together.
